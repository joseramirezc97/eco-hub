Core Colors & Usage

Current Version (v2.3)

The secondary palette utilizes a set of 5 muted tones including slate grey, charcoal, and warm taupe. These should be used in a 60/30/10 ratio to maintain visual hierarchy across digital interfaces.

Draft Version (v2.4)

The secondary palette has been expanded to include Coca-Cola Red (#EF3340) as a high-visibility accent color. This accent should be reserved for interactive elements, call-to-actions, and structural highlights. The new usage ratio follows a 70/20/10 distribution with increased emphasis on white space.
